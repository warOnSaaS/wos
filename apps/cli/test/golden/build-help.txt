Usage: wos build [options] <abu>

LEASE, BUILD, VERIFY, submit, then wait through CI, REVIEW, QUALIFY and PR

Arguments:
  abu              ABU id, or <target>/<abu-key> (e.g. salesforce/contacts#04)

Options:
  --model <model>  builder model (D15), default opus (choices: "opus", "astra",
                   "sol")
  --detach         stop after local verify and submit; `wos resume` re-attaches
  -h, --help       display help for command
