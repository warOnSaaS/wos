## Scope

The HubSpot customer platform at catalog level: Smart CRM (Starter/Professional/Enterprise), Free Tools, Marketing Hub, Sales Hub, Service Hub, Content Hub, Data Hub and Revenue Hub (renamed from Commerce Hub; Professional and Enterprise only), plus Breeze AI agents, as listed in HubSpot's Product & Services Catalog.

Left out of this scan:

- HubSpot Work (Beta) and Work agents: beta project/document workspace, not scanned beyond its catalog row
- AEO (Beta) as a standalone product: folded into seo-tools only
- Small Business Bundle and seat/credit pricing: packaging, not capabilities
- Consulting, onboarding, training and migration services: services, not software
- HubSpot Academy, Community and partner (Solutions Partner) programme: not product capabilities
- Legacy pricing models and legacy (pre-2024) editions
- HubSpot Capital financing (powered by Stripe): financing offer, not a software capability
- Clearbit/Breeze Intelligence as a separate brand: covered only as data-enrichment and buyer-intent rows

## Public API

**Style:** REST (JSON) with date-based versions (e.g. /crm/objects/2026-09/...), OpenAPI specs; webhooks and a webhooks journal; UI extensions; remote and developer MCP servers

Auth by OAuth for multi-account apps, static access tokens for single-account apps, service keys (Beta) and personal access keys for the CLI. Catalog limits: 100 requests/10s and 250,000 calls/day on Free and Starter, 190/10s and 650,000/day on Professional, 1,000,000/day on Enterprise, raisable with API Limit Increases.

## Getting data out

How a customer gets their data out of this product, from public sources: facts for a future shared import engine, not a design.

| Full extraction | Incremental (delta) | By API |
|---|---|---|
| partial | yes | yes |

Core CRM records, properties, associations, activities, files and conversations are readable by UI export and REST APIs, but the account backup excludes associations and activity data, property history is capped at 45 (contacts) or 20 (other objects) revisions, and workflow exports omit history and email data. Deltas are available via search filters on lastmodifieddate/hs_lastmodifieddate, webhooks (creation, deletion, merge, restore, propertyChange, associationChange) and a webhooks journal holding the past three days of events.

### Auth for a third-party importer

**Models:** oauth-app, personal-token, other

Apps distributed to multiple accounts must use OAuth (distribution 'marketplace', or 'private' for up to 10 allowlisted accounts) with a self-hosted OAuth backend; single-account apps use a static auth access token (distribution 'private'); client credentials tokens serve only the webhooks journal API; personal access keys authenticate the CLI. The exports API's crm.export scope can only be granted over OAuth by a Super Admin. Marketplace listing requires error responses under 5% of daily requests.

Sources: [1](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/apps/developer-platform/build-apps/authentication/overview.md) [2](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/exports/guide.md) [3](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/developer-tooling/platform/usage-guidelines.md)

### Rate limits and quotas

Marketplace-distributed OAuth apps: 110 requests every 10 seconds per installing account (excluding CRM Search), not raised by the API limit increase. Privately distributed apps: Free/Starter 100 per 10s per app and 250,000/day per account; Professional 190/10s and 625,000/day; Enterprise 190/10s and 1,000,000/day; with API Limit Increase 250/10s and +1,000,000/day per increase (max two). Daily limit resets at midnight account time zone; 429 on breach. CRM Search: 5 requests/second per account, 10,000 results per query. Associations: 500,000/day on Professional and Enterprise (max 1,000,000 with increase), burst 100/10s Free/Starter, 150/10s Pro/Enterprise (200 with increase). Exports API: 30 exports per rolling 24h, one at a time. Webhooks journal: 100 requests/second per app.

Sources: [1](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/developer-tooling/platform/usage-guidelines.md) [2](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/search-the-crm.md) [3](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/associations/associate-records/guide.md) [4](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/crm/exports/guide.md) [5](https://developers.hubspot.com/docs/api-reference/latest/webhooks-journal/guide.md)
