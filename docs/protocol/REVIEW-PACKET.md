## 1. Summary (self-contained)

warOnSaaS's coordinator, wOS, leases small build units to contributors whose own Claude/ChatGPT subscriptions run the agents; two other contributors' agents (Astra on Codex, Fable on Claude) and a human review the work; the wOS GitHub App merges it. Amendment 02 adds an economy: **WOS**, a Solana token (devnet in V1), allocated by **Proof of Contribution**.

The design:
- **Receipts.** A merged unit produces an immutable ContributionReceipt. Execution work is weighted by the agent's provider usage, normalised to **ACU** by a versioned oracle (1 ACU = USD 1 of list-price API inference, never shown as money), **clipped at the unit's cap** (peer P75 × 1.25). Usage is **ATTESTED at best** (client-reported); run logs with exact per-turn totals are required; mainnet eligibility of attested usage fails closed until the founder decides. Planning, proposals, bugs, security and human review carry outcome weights (ACU-equivalents).
- **Epochs (weekly).** Budget = 3,327 ppm of the remaining 995M-WOS reserve (≈ 4-year half-life), split 60/10/5/5/15/5 (execution, planning, human review, outcomes, completion accrual, security reserve). Distributing slices emit at most a **rate ceiling** (100 WOS/ACU, decaying) × weight; the rest returns to the reserve; accruals scale with utilisation. One conserved equation: reserve + pools + security reserve + issued = 995M.
- **Optimistic payouts.** CALCULATING (48 h: sampled audits 5%, canaries, bounded holds) → PROPOSED (every allocation public with an explanation and deterministic anomaly metrics; 48 h challenge window; silence accepts) → FINALIZED. Any epoch participant can dispute any set of allocations (stake 2%/item, cap 10%); the dispute focuses a sealed payout-audit gate of 2 random outside-feature auditors run on auditors' own subscriptions; outcomes UPHELD/CLIPPED/REVOKED; bounty 20% of total excess. After finalization, corrections are offsets against future allocations, never on-chain reversals.
- **Bootstrap.** The founder may merge anything (public label) but his own work gets PROVISIONAL receipts that count only in devnet test epochs until independently ratified. Genesis credit for pre-protocol work is valued by accepted output only (retro size points × reference ACU × early rate), capped at 0.5% of supply, issued only at mainnet with 2-year vesting, approved by ≥ 2 non-founder humans.
- **Solana.** Token-2022 with metadata only; no freeze, no permanent delegate, no transfer hook. Devnet: claim-triggered push transfers (retry-safe by blockhash expiry, one confirmed transfer per leaf in the DB), epoch roots anchored by SPL Memo, no custom programs. Mainnet: fund an audited emission escrow and revoke the mint; migration (off-ramp) via a new mint or credits from a public snapshot.
- **Governance.** Policy numbers are data, forward-only, previewed by the simulation. Founder mode until an activation threshold; then tiered supermajorities (60/70/75%, emergency > 50%) in BOTH locked-token (≥ 12 months) and 6-month contribution weight, denominators over distributed supply only, 10% cap per organization.
- **Organizations** can be beneficiaries; org-mates are related accounts for every independence rule.

Artifacts: 17 design documents, draft contracts that typecheck with 41 tests, draft migration 0007 applied in `db:test` with 70+ behavioural assertions, a deterministic simulation whose tables are regenerated by the test suite.

## 4. Questions for you to attack (ranked by what worries the architect most)

1. **Usage fabrication economics.** Given caps at peer P75 × 1.25, exact-total run logs, a 50% haircut for bare numbers, anomaly ranking, disputes with a 20% total-excess bounty, 5% sampled audits and a 13-epoch pattern lookback: is the expected gain from fabricated-but-consistent usage really ≈ 0 (TOKENOMICS-SIMULATION A assumes the lookback recovers what it finds)? What breaks it?
2. **Conservation and the engine.** Find any path in `engine.ts` (disputes, offsets, pool payouts, security payouts, utilisation-scaled accruals, returns) that violates R + P + S + I = reserve, double-counts, or lets rounding be steered. Is per-receipt largest-remainder splitting gameable by splitting weight across many receipts?
3. **The rate ceiling.** Does "min(slice, ceiling × weight)" with unused budget returning to the remaining reserve create any timing game (e.g. waiting for quiet epochs, or flooding one epoch)? Should the ceiling be per ACU or per merged size point?
4. **Optimistic verification.** With silence = accept and 48 h windows, what does a colluding group need to pass inflated allocations? Is the dispute stake low enough to use and high enough to stop griefing (table G)? Is epoch-wide standing abusable (retaliation, extortion: "pay me or I dispute")?
5. **DB enforcement in 0007.** Find a way to violate: receipt immutability, the receipt status machine, epoch windows (note the `at` column is set by the trigger), exactly-once inclusion, one confirmed settlement per leaf, related-account independence, wallet uniqueness, forward-only policy activation, or the pause expiry. Check SECURITY DEFINER use in `check_dispute_item` and RLS on every new table.
6. **Canaries.** Are payout canaries actually indistinguishable during the audit window given what is public (receipts are public; usage is published only after finalization)? What would a careful modified client use to tell them apart?
7. **Bootstrap and Genesis.** Can the founder (or anyone) turn PROVISIONAL work, test epochs or Genesis into value outside the stated rules? Is "accepted output only" Genesis valuation sound?
8. **Solana.** Is Token-2022 with metadata only the right mint? Is the claim-triggered push design retry-safe as written? Is M1 (escrow + revoke) implementable with audited programs, and is the off-ramp without a retained mint authority complete?
9. **Governance.** Can tiered dual supermajorities over distributed denominators be captured early (table K) or gamed by lock timing, organizations or Sybil contribution weight? Is excluding unclaimed allocations from denominators right?
10. **Privacy and legal exposure** of publishing every allocation (pseudonym + wallet + usage after finalization) and of organization beneficiaries.
